Also supply compatible closed-cell foam, thin driver gaskets, removable sealant, porous lining and wire. All panel assembly uses M3 screws; no structural glue is required. Select the actual washers to give the modelled 1.5 mm head-side metal/cushion stack. Inserts are 4 mm long in 4.2 mm pilots. Test retention and tighten gently; no torque rating has been physically qualified. Binding posts use their own supplied hardware.
